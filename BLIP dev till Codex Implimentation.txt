1. Project Vision
Purpose

BLIP (Business Logic Intelligence Platform) is an enterprise-grade investigation and business analysis platform that helps organizations identify operational problems, investigate root causes, validate hypotheses with evidence, and generate actionable recommendations using structured analytics and AI assistance.

The platform is designed to bridge traditional Business Analysis, Six Sigma, Lean methodologies, and modern AI-driven investigation workflows.

Long-Term Goals

The product aims to become an enterprise operating intelligence platform capable of:

Business problem investigation
Root cause analysis
Evidence management
KPI monitoring
Executive dashboards
AI-assisted analytics
AI-generated recommendations
Knowledge management
Organization-wide investigation repository
Decision intelligence

Eventually BLIP should evolve into a SaaS product.

16. Lessons Learned
Validate the application by running it before adding new features; startup errors are easier to fix incrementally than after multiple changes.
Keep business logic in the service layer and database access in the CRUD layer to maintain clear separation of concerns.
Use UUID primary keys throughout for consistency and scalability.
Prefer idempotent seed scripts so environments can be initialized repeatedly without duplicate data.
Avoid guessing project structure during implementation; verify actual module locations (such as the database session file) before generating code.
Build on a stable foundation first (authentication, database, architecture) before adding advanced features like RBAC or AI.
